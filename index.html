A note on protein: The calculator uses ICMR–NIN’s general adult RDA of 0.83 g/kg/day as a rough estimate. BMI is calculated from height and weight, but BMI does not determine protein needs and cannot distinguish muscle from fat. This estimate is for adults; it is not intended for children, pregnancy or breastfeeding, or people with kidney disease or other conditions affecting nutrition. Ask a qualified health professional for personal advice. Pairing pulses with cereals can help improve protein quality in a vegetarian diet.
